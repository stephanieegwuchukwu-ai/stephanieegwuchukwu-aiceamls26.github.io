what_i_learned: |
    Today taught me the importance of preparation, teamwork, and effective communication. Research is not only about collecting data and building models—it is also about being able to clearly explain your work, defend your decisions, and demonstrate the impact of your findings. Presenting at the symposium strengthened my confidence in public speaking and reminded me that practice and preparation truly make a difference. Looking back, I am grateful for the opportunity to present the research that my team and I have worked so hard on throughout the program. It was exciting to finally share the progress we have made, from developing our baseline ECG classification model to analyzing its performance and discussing the importance of fairness in AI systems. The symposium gave me the chance to present my assigned sections, explain our data and preliminary findings, and answer questions from faculty, mentors, and fellow researchers. Seeing all of the preparation come together made me appreciate how much I have learned over the course of this program. Beyond the technical skills of training machine learning models and interpreting results, I have also improved my ability to communicate complex ideas in a way that others can understand. Throughout the presentations, I enjoyed listening to the other research teams and learning about the different projects being conducted across the program. It was inspiring to see the variety of research topics and the unique approaches each team used to solve real-world problems. The symposium created an environment where everyone could learn from one another while celebrating the progress we had made.


blockers: |
    My biggest challenge today was managing my nerves before presenting. Although I knew the material well, standing in front of an audience made me anxious. Once I started speaking, however, I became more comfortable and was able to focus on explaining the research rather than worrying about making mistakes.

reflection: |
  I was definitely nervous before it was my turn to present, but I thank God that everything went well. Looking back, I realize that all the time spent preparing, revising slides, and practicing was worth it. I am proud of how my team worked together and how we represented our project at the symposium.
