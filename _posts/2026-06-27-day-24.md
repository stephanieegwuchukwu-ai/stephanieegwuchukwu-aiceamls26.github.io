what_i_learned: |
     Today was dedicated almost entirely to preparing for the Mid-Summer Symposium. I revised my presentation slides, carefully reviewing the key information in the data collection, preliminary findings, and data analysis sections to ensure everything was accurate and easy to explain. I also spent time refining my demo speech so it would sound more natural and confident. After lunch, I continued updating my slides based on the feedback and suggestions from my mentor. Later in the day, my team and I presented our research to two other groups, which gave me valuable practice explaining our work and receiving feedback. We ended the day with a Zoom meeting led by Dr. Nyarko, the Director of the CEAMLS SAIRI program, where we discussed and just reflected on our experiences with the AI program.

blockers: |
    The main challenge today was time. Because I spent most of the day revising my slides and incorporating feedback, I did not have enough time to practice presenting my full presentation as much as I would have liked before the group presentation. Luckily, this was just a practice round so I have the whole weekend to prep for the actual presentation. Also, I would have loved a feedback on my group's performance during the practice today, but we had to hop on a zoom meeting when we were about rounding up our presentation.

reflection: |
    Today’s work reinforced that a strong presentation requires more than just good research—it also takes careful organization, clear communication, and continuous revision. Presenting to other groups helped me identify areas where I can improve my delivery, and the feedback from my mentor strengthened the overall quality of my slides, so I can deliver it with greater confidence at the Mid-Summer Symposium.
