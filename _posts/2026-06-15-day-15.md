what_i_learned: |
  Today was our first in-person day of the program, and I learned a lot from both Dr. Dacon and Dr. Valerie. During Dr. Dacon's session, we worked in Google Colab and created a DataFrame while learning more about machine learning concepts such as correlation, confusion matrices, True and false positives, True and false negatives, precision, recall, and F1 scores. I learned that a model with high accuracy is not always the best model if it performs poorly on other evaluation metrics. It was interesting to see how different measures help us better understand a model's strengths and weaknesses. We also explored AI bias and fairness through group activities and real-world examples in healthcare, hiring, and facial recognition. These conversations helped me understand why it is so important to have diverse datasets and design AI systems that work fairly for different groups of people. I learned the difference between trust and trustworthiness in AI, and why we should evaluate AI tools for fairness, reliability, and accuracy before relying on them. I also enjoyed comparing how different AI tools responded to the same prompt. It was interesting to see that even when given the exact same information, different models could come to different conclusions, which showed me how much data, assumptions, and model design can influence AI outputs.
