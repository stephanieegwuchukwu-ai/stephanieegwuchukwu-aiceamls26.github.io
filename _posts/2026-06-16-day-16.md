I prepared the PTB-XL dataset by cleaning the metadata, mapping the diagnostic codes into the five superclass categories, and creating the training, validation, and test splits. I spent time understanding the training pipeline, including how the model makes predictions, calculates loss, computes gradients, and updates its weights during learning. I also worked on understanding the Hybrid CNN-Transformer architecture and what each part of the code is doing, which helped me better understand how the model works. I learned more about fairness evaluation techniques and how to measure whether the model performs differently across demographic groups. I explored inverse frequency weighting as a method for reducing bias by giving more attention to underrepresented groups during training. I also learned why training with multiple random seeds is important for producing reliable and reproducible research results. Finally, I learned how to save trained models to Google Drive, verify that they were saved correctly, and manage model files for future evaluation and experimentation.
