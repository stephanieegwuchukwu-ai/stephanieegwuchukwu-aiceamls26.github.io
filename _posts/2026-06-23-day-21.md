what_i_learned: |
    Today, I learned more about why the code uses multiple Conv1d layers in the baseline CNN. Each layer captures increasingly complex patterns in the ECG signal, from small wave features to more abstract diagnostic structures. I also learned how multi-label targets are converted into separate binary columns, how NumPy arrays make subgroup filtering easier, and how dictionaries are used to store metadata such as age and sex. Most importantly, I learned that the project is not only about accuracy but also about fairness across age and sex groups, as well as interpretability through Grad-CAM. I also learned how to differentiate between the baseline model and the weighted mitigation model. The baseline model trains normally, while the principled inverse-frequency weighting approach increases the influence of underrepresented demographic groups during training. This makes the fairness aspect of the project more meaningful because the model is being encouraged to pay greater attention to groups that might otherwise be overlooked.
