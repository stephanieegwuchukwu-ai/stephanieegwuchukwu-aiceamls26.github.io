what_i_learned: |
 Today was one of those days that actually really pushed me to grow as a researcher and AI coder. It started off strong with Dr. Pandey's informative session on literature reviews. We went over the PEAC Checklist, which stands for Professionalism, Ethicality, Accuracy, Clarity, and Completeness. It remindered me about how to actually makes a presentation or a paper look professional and trustworthy. During this session, I started mapping out my own research outline. I also put together a Venn diagram for the keywords and topics I want to dive into. Doing that made my ideas feel a lot more organized, and I started seeing connections I hadn't noticed before. Later on, we did this interactive Amazon Kahoot about live streaming tech. I picked up a few new computer science terms like latency, redundancy, Content Delivery Networks(CDNs), etc. It was interesting to see how AI and machine learning are used to detect issues and cut down on delays, to make the whole viewer experience smoother. 
 In Python 101, we spent more time on conditionals. We practiced using if, elif, and else to build programs that can actually make decisions. We also got into functions, which are basically just named sets of instructions for specific tasks. We looked at functions with return values also, and I learned that the return statement is usually the very last thing in a function because it is what sends the data back to the rest of the program.
