what_i_learned: |
  One thing that actually stuck with me today was learning that there are different way and paths in doing research. I learned research and AI intersect together.  In the literature review lecture with Dr Pandey, we talked about this idea of researching to write and writing to research. Basically, the writing and the actual digging for informations in a way feed into each other the whole time you're working on a project and that you don't do one and then the other. I learned about tools like Rayyan and NotebookLM which I believe helps make the literature review part way faster, especially since we are going to be trying to sort through a lot of articles. 
 I also finally understood the 80/10/10 data split which is used to train the model, monitor its performance, and evaluate how well it generalizes to unseen ECG data. I learned that you use 80% to train the model, 10% for validation, and the last 10% for the actual testing which is basically just the way we'll be able to figure out if the AI is actually learning anything or just guessing. We also looked at how the code works, I and my team individually reviewed the data prepartion pipeline codes which was given by my grad mentor, and he also explained and responded to the questions I asked. Overall, I feel like I finally have a better sense of all the behind the scenes work that happens before an AI model even starts.
