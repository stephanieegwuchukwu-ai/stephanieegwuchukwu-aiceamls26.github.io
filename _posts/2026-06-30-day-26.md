what_i_learned: |
    Today, I learned that preparing students for the future requires more than simply introducing AI tools into the classroom. During the breakout session EDU302 – Building an AI-Ready University: From Campus to Career Readiness, the speakers emphasized the importance of creating AI-ready institutions. They explained that universities should integrate AI across teaching, learning, research, student services, and career preparation so that AI becomes a meaningful part of the educational experience. The discussion focused on how institutions can equip students with the knowledge and practical skills needed to thrive in an AI-driven world while encouraging the responsible and effective use of AI. Beyond the sessions, I explored the expo, learned about a variety of companies and emerging technologies, and expanded my professional network by connecting with several interesting professionals on LinkedIn, including David, who is also an intern at the Department of Homeland Security. I also joined the AWS Student Certification Voucher Giveaway to explore the opportunity to earn an AWS certification. Overall, the summit showed me the value of networking, staying curious, and taking advantage of opportunities that support both personal and professional growth.
